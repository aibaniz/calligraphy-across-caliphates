## Meet the Team

*Calligraphy Across the Caliphates* was developed by a collaborative team of junior researchers within the Seminar for Arabic Studies at Freie Universität Berlin, under the guidance of Dr. Christian Casey. The project explores Islamic art through the lens of digital humanities, with a focus on accessibility, historical accuracy, and visual storytelling.

- [Aibaniz Alieva](https://aibaniz.github.io/cv/) – Project Lead – Research, Visual Identity & Frontend Development  
- [Iuliia Goriacheva](https://judegor.github.io/cv_iu/) – Research, Geodata Curation & Interactive Map Development

We are grateful to our mentors and the department for their support.
